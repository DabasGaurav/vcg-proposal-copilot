# Request for Proposal — Retail Lending Transformation

## Client

ABC Bank, a retail bank operating across India.

## Background

ABC Bank's consumer lending approval process is slow and inconsistent across
branches. Applications pass through multiple manual handoffs and the bank is
losing volume to digital-first lenders. ABC Bank seeks a partner to redesign
its lending operations and prove the impact in a time-boxed pilot.

## Timeline

The engagement must be delivered within 12 weeks, including a measured branch
pilot before any rollout recommendation.

## Scope of Work

- Diagnose the current origination-to-disbursement process and quantify delay
  at each step.
- Redesign the credit-decision workflow, including auto-decisioning for
  low-risk applications.
- Redesign the branch and back-office operating model to support the new
  workflow.
- Align credit policy and governance with the redesigned process.
- Define and run a controlled pilot in a limited set of branches.
- Produce a rollout plan and benefits tracking mechanism.

## Deliverables

- Current-state diagnostic with a quantified opportunity map.
- Target operating model and detailed future-state process designs.
- Pilot plan with success metrics and a matched baseline.
- Validated pilot results pack.
- National rollout plan.
- Benefits tracking framework.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Demonstrated experience redesigning retail lending operations for a bank.
- Demonstrated measurable results from a comparable lending engagement.
- Evidence of greater than 30 percent turnaround-time improvement in a
  comparable lending engagement.
- A credible 12-week workplan that reaches a measured pilot.
- Named team members with relevant lending operations experience.
- A clear view of delivery risks and how they will be managed.

## Procedural Requirements

- Proposals must be submitted in English only.
- Commercial pricing must be submitted as a separate sealed document.
- Two client references for comparable engagements must be provided.
