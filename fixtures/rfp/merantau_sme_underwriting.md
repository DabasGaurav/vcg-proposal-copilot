# Request for Proposal — SME Underwriting Process Improvement

## Client

Bank Merantau, a mid-size commercial bank operating across Southeast Asia.

## Background

Bank Merantau's SME book is growing but underwriting capacity has not kept
pace. A backlog has built up and credit decisions are inconsistent between
relationship managers. The bank seeks a partner to improve the underwriting
process and build a credible capacity model.

## Timeline

The engagement must be delivered within 14 weeks.

## Scope of Work

- Diagnose the SME underwriting process and quantify rework.
- Standardise the credit assessment template.
- Introduce checklist-based document intake.
- Separate simple renewals from new-to-bank underwriting.
- Define a workload-balancing routine for the underwriting queue.
- Build an underwriting capacity model for the credit team.

## Deliverables

- Underwriting process diagnostic with quantified rework.
- Standardised credit assessment template.
- Document intake checklist and intake process design.
- Underwriting capacity and workload-balancing model.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Demonstrated experience improving SME underwriting for a bank.
- Demonstrated measurable reduction in underwriting rework from a comparable
  engagement.
- Demonstrated experience in the Southeast Asian market.
- Named team members with SME underwriting process design experience.
- A clear view of delivery risks and how they will be managed.

## Procedural Requirements

- Proposals must be submitted in English.
- Commercial pricing must be submitted as a separate document.
