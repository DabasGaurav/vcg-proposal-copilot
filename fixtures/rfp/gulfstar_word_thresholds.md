# Request for Proposal — Retail Credit Operations Improvement

## Client

GulfStar Bank, a retail bank operating across the Middle East.

## Background

GulfStar's retail credit operations are slower and more manual than regional
peers. The bank seeks a partner to redesign the process and prove impact in a
pilot.

## Timeline

The engagement must be delivered within sixteen weeks.

## Scope of Work

- Diagnose the retail credit origination process.
- Redesign the credit-decision workflow.
- Run a controlled pilot in a limited set of branches.

## Deliverables

- Origination process diagnostic.
- Redesigned credit-decision workflow.
- Pilot results pack with a matched baseline.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Demonstrated experience redesigning retail credit operations for a bank.
- Evidence of a turnaround-time reduction of more than twenty percent in a
  comparable engagement.
- Evidence of a forecast accuracy improvement of at least fifteen percentage
  points in a comparable engagement.
- Demonstrated prior delivery experience in the Middle East market.
- Named team members with retail credit operations experience.

## Procedural Requirements

- Proposals must be submitted in English.
