# Request for Proposal — Supply Chain Planning Operating Model

## Client

Nutriva Foods, a packaged-foods manufacturer operating across India.

## Background

Nutriva's service levels to modern-trade customers are volatile. Inventory is
simultaneously too high in some categories and stocked out in others. Regional
planning teams work to disconnected plans and decision rights are unclear.
Nutriva seeks a partner to redesign its planning operating model.

## Timeline

The engagement must be completed within 14 weeks.

## Scope of Work

- Diagnose the current demand and supply planning process.
- Design a single monthly planning cycle with clear decision rights.
- Redesign demand planning, supply planning and distribution requirements
  planning.
- Define a forecast accuracy measurement baseline.
- Recommend an inventory cover target by category.

## Deliverables

- Current-state planning diagnostic.
- Target planning operating model with decision rights.
- Forecast accuracy baseline and measurement framework.
- Inventory cover recommendation by category.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Demonstrated experience redesigning a supply chain planning operating model.
- Demonstrated measurable forecast accuracy improvement from a comparable
  engagement.
- Demonstrated measurable inventory reduction from a comparable engagement.
- Named team members with supply chain planning experience.
- A clear view of delivery risks and how they will be managed.

## Procedural Requirements

- Proposals must be submitted in English.
- Commercial pricing must be submitted separately.
