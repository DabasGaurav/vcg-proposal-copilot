# Request for Proposal — Credit Operations Improvement (Procurement-Governed)

## Client

PQR Bank, a commercial bank.

## Background

PQR Bank seeks a partner to improve the efficiency and consistency of its
credit operations. This procurement is governed by the bank's standard vendor
onboarding rules and all submission requirements below are mandatory and
pass/fail.

## Timeline

The engagement is expected to run for 12 weeks.

## Scope of Work

- Diagnose bottlenecks in the credit operations process.
- Redesign the underwriting workflow and document intake.
- Define a credit team capacity and workload-balancing model.
- Recommend a set of process controls to improve decision consistency.

## Deliverables

- Process diagnostic with quantified bottlenecks.
- Redesigned underwriting workflow.
- Capacity and workload model.
- Controls recommendation.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Demonstrated experience improving bank credit or underwriting operations.
- Demonstrated measurable process improvement from a comparable engagement.
- Named team with underwriting process experience.

## Procedural Requirements

- A signed vendor declaration on the bank's template must accompany the proposal.
- Proposals must use 11-point Arial, single spacing, and must not exceed 20 pages.
- Two client references, each with a named contact, must be provided.
- The commercial response must be submitted on the bank's pricing template as a
  separate file and must not appear anywhere in the technical proposal.
- Proposals must be submitted through the bank's e-procurement portal before the
  stated deadline; email submissions are rejected.
