# Request for Proposal — Lending Turnaround Acceleration

## Client

Apex Bank, a retail bank operating across India.

## Background

Apex Bank's consumer lending approval turnaround is uncompetitive. The bank has
set aggressive internal targets and will only shortlist partners who can
evidence results at that level from prior work.

## Timeline

The engagement must be delivered within 12 weeks.

## Scope of Work

- Diagnose the origination-to-disbursement process and quantify delay.
- Redesign the credit-decision workflow with auto-decisioning.
- Run a controlled pilot against a matched baseline.

## Deliverables

- Current-state diagnostic with a quantified opportunity map.
- Redesigned credit-decision workflow.
- Pilot plan and validated pilot results pack.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Evidence of at least 45 percent turnaround-time improvement in a comparable
  lending engagement.
- Evidence of at least 50 percent reduction in processing effort per
  application in a comparable lending engagement.
- Evidence of more than 60 percent reduction in manual handoffs in a
  comparable lending engagement.
- Named team members with relevant lending operations experience.
- A clear view of delivery risks and how they will be managed.

## Procedural Requirements

- Proposals must be submitted in English.
