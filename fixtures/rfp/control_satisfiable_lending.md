# Request for Proposal — Retail Lending Process Improvement

## Client

Sahyog Bank, a retail bank operating across India.

## Background

Sahyog Bank's consumer lending approval process is slow and inconsistent across
branches. The bank seeks a partner to redesign the process and prove the impact
in a measured pilot.

## Timeline

The engagement must be delivered within 12 weeks.

## Scope of Work

- Diagnose the origination-to-disbursement process and quantify delay.
- Redesign the credit-decision workflow, including auto-decisioning for
  low-risk applications.
- Define and run a controlled pilot in a limited set of branches.

## Deliverables

- Current-state diagnostic with a quantified opportunity map.
- Target operating model and future-state process designs.
- Validated pilot results pack.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Demonstrated experience redesigning retail lending operations for a bank.
- Evidence of more than 15 percent turnaround-time improvement in a comparable
  lending engagement.
- Evidence of more than 20 percent reduction in manual handoffs in a comparable
  lending engagement.
- Named team members with relevant lending operations experience.

## Procedural Requirements

- Proposals must be submitted in English.
