# Ananya Mehta — Partner

## Summary

Ananya Mehta is a Partner at VCG with 18 years of experience in banking and
lending operations. She leads the firm's retail lending practice in India.

## Experience

- Led the retail lending operations redesign for a large Indian bank, including
  a measured branch pilot.
- Advised two other banks on credit-decision workflow and auto-decisioning
  design.
- Prior to consulting, spent six years in retail credit operations at a
  commercial bank.

## Focus Areas

Origination-to-disbursement redesign, auto-decisioning, branch operating model,
credit policy alignment.

## Education

MBA, finance concentration. Undergraduate degree in economics.
