# Winning Proposal Patterns — Credit Operations Engagements

## What Reviewers Rewarded

- A workplan that reaches a measured pilot within a quarter, not a year-long
  study.
- Success criteria expressed in the client's own metric language.
- A risks section that names the real delivery risks: data availability for
  baselining, branch change fatigue, credit-risk appetite for auto-decisioning.
- Team pages with named individuals and honest time commitments.

## Reusable Framing

- Problem framing: fragmented approval process, manual handoffs, slow
  turnaround, volume leakage to faster competitors.
- Approach framing: value-stream diagnosis, tiered auto-decisioning, exception
  handling, operating-model redesign, controlled pilot with matched baseline.
- Outcome framing: turnaround, processing effort, handoff count, with risk cost
  held flat.

## Commercial Framing

Commercial sections state scope, phasing, and assumptions. The fee figure and
payment schedule are inserted by the engagement partner and are never produced
from a template or an estimate.
