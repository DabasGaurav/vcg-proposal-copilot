# SME Lending Process Improvement — Southeast Asian Bank

## Engagement Context

A mid-size Southeast Asian bank asked VCG to improve its small and medium
enterprise (SME) lending process. The bank's SME book was growing but
underwriting capacity had not kept pace, creating a backlog and inconsistent
credit decisions between relationship managers.

The engagement ran for fourteen weeks and covered process diagnosis, a redesign
of the underwriting workflow, and a capacity model for the credit team.

## Approach

VCG standardised the credit assessment template, introduced a checklist-based
document intake, and separated simple renewals from new-to-bank underwriting.
A workload-balancing routine was introduced for the underwriting queue.

## Measured Results

Results were measured on process metrics during a six-week observation period.

- Rework loops in underwriting fell by 25 percent.
- Time spent by relationship managers on document chasing fell by 17 percent.
- Decision consistency, scored on a sample audit, improved materially.

No validated end-to-end turnaround-time reduction was recorded for this
engagement. The observation window was too short and the bank's core system did
not timestamp the intermediate steps, so a turnaround figure could not be
substantiated. This engagement should not be cited as evidence of a
turnaround-time result.

## Notes

This was an SME lending engagement in Southeast Asia. It is a process-quality
reference, not a turnaround reference, and not an Indian-market reference.
