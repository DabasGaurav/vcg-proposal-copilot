# Supply Chain Operating Model Transformation — Consumer Goods Company

## Engagement Context

A consumer-goods manufacturer in India engaged VCG on a supply-chain operating
model transformation. Service levels to modern-trade customers were volatile and
inventory was simultaneously too high in some categories and stocked out in
others.

## Approach

VCG redesigned the planning operating model, moving from disconnected regional
plans to a single monthly planning cycle with clear decision rights. The
transformation covered demand planning, supply planning, and distribution
requirements planning.

## Results

- Forecast accuracy at the category level improved by 12 points.
- Finished-goods inventory cover fell by nine days.
- Case-fill rate to key accounts rose above the target threshold.

## Notes

This engagement is about physical supply chain and planning. It contains no
banking, lending, credit, or financial-services content. The words
"operating model", "transformation", and "India" appear here but the domain is
consumer-goods distribution, not banking operations.
