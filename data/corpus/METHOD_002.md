# Core Banking Diagnostic Framework

## Purpose

A structured framework for assessing the health of a bank's core banking
platform and the operating processes built on top of it. It produces a
diagnostic, not a delivery track record.

## Assessment Dimensions

- Platform architecture and technical debt.
- Process coupling to the core, and the cost of change.
- Data model fitness for lending, deposits, and payments.
- Integration surface and channel enablement.
- Operational resilience and recovery posture.

## Method

Structured interviews, artefact review, and a capability self-assessment scored
against a reference model. A heat map of risk and opportunity is produced with
an indicative remediation roadmap.

## Important Scope Note

This framework demonstrates diagnostic capability only. It is not a record of
delivered lending transformation and must not be cited as evidence that VCG has
executed a past lending delivery engagement.
