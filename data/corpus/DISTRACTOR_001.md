# VCG Office Sustainability Initiative

## Background

VCG's facilities team ran a twelve-month sustainability initiative across its
office locations to cut energy consumption and reduce operating cost.

## Actions Taken

- Replaced legacy lighting with LED fixtures on motion sensors.
- Re-commissioned building HVAC schedules around actual occupancy.
- Consolidated server-room load and raised the set-point.
- Switched two sites to a renewable electricity tariff.

## Results

- Electricity use across the measured sites fell by 35 percent year on year.
- Paper consumption fell by 40 percent after a default-duplex print policy.
- The initiative paid back its capital cost in under two years.

## Notes

This is an internal facilities initiative. It has no client, no banking or
lending content, and no engagement delivery record. The 35 percent figure
refers to office electricity consumption only.
