# VCG Lending Operations Transformation Methodology

## Overview

A twelve-week, four-phase methodology for redesigning lending operations. It is
designed to reach a measured pilot inside a single quarter.

## Phase 1 — Diagnose (Weeks 1-3)

Value-stream mapping of origination to disbursement, queue-time quantification,
handoff and rework analysis, and a baseline of turnaround time and cost to
serve. Output: a prioritised opportunity map and a target operating model
hypothesis.

## Phase 2 — Design (Weeks 4-7)

Redesign of the credit-decision workflow, auto-decisioning tiers, exception
handling, credit policy alignment, and the branch and back-office operating
model. Output: detailed process designs, role definitions, and a pilot plan.

## Phase 3 — Pilot (Weeks 8-11)

Controlled pilot in a limited set of branches against a matched baseline.
Weekly measurement of turnaround time, processing effort, handoffs, and risk
indicators. Output: a validated results pack.

## Phase 4 — Scale (Week 12)

Rollout plan, capability transfer, governance handover, and a benefits
tracking mechanism. Output: a board-ready scale recommendation.

## Measurement Discipline

Every claimed improvement is measured against a matched baseline. Turnaround
figures are only reported where intermediate process steps are timestamped.
