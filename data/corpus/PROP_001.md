# Winning Proposal Skeleton — Lending Operations Engagement

## Standard Section Structure

1. Executive Summary — the client's ask, our understanding, headline approach,
   why VCG, expected outcome. Written last.
2. Context and Problem Understanding — restate the client situation in their
   terms; show we have read the RFP.
3. Objectives and Success Criteria — mirror the RFP evaluation criteria.
4. Proposed Approach and Workplan — phased, week-numbered, with outputs per
   phase.
5. Team — named roles, relevant experience, time commitment.
6. Relevant Experience and Credentials — case references with measured results.
7. Risks and Mitigations — the three or four risks that actually matter.
8. Commercial — pricing and commercial terms (client-facing pricing is inserted
   by the engagement partner, never drafted by template).
9. Assumptions and Ways of Working — access, cadence, decision rights.

## Tone Patterns

- Specific over grand: name the metric, the baseline, the method.
- Prospective language for what we will do ("We propose to...", "In weeks 1-3
  the team will...").
- Past-tense claims only where there is a cited reference behind them.
- No superlatives about VCG that a reference cannot support.
