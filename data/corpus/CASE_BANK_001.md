# Retail Lending Operations Redesign — Large Indian Bank

## Engagement Context

A large Indian retail bank engaged VCG to redesign its consumer lending
operations across personal loans and two-wheeler finance. The bank was losing
volume to digital-first competitors because branch-originated applications moved
slowly through a fragmented approval process with many manual handoffs.

The engagement ran for sixteen weeks, structured as diagnostic, redesign, and a
controlled pilot in twelve branches in two regions.

## Approach

VCG mapped the end-to-end origination-to-disbursement value stream, quantified
queue time at each step, and rebuilt the credit-decision workflow around a
tiered auto-decisioning model. Low-risk applications were routed to a
straight-through path; only exceptions reached a human underwriter.

Governance, credit policy, and the branch operating model were redesigned
together so the faster workflow did not raise risk cost.

## Measured Results

The pilot was measured against a matched baseline of comparable branches over
the preceding two quarters.

- Pilot approval turnaround time was reduced by 18 percent versus the baseline.
- Processing effort per application fell by 22 percent.
- Manual handoffs per application dropped by 30 percent, from ten steps to seven.
- Credit loss rate in the pilot cohort was unchanged within measurement error.

The bank approved a phased national rollout on the strength of the pilot.

## Lessons

Turnaround improvement came primarily from removing handoffs and rework, not
from cutting underwriting scrutiny. Attempts to push the auto-decision
threshold higher during the pilot were reverted because early-warning risk
indicators moved.
